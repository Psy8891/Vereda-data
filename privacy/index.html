Vereda privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Vereda is a hiking app for Madeira and Porto Santo. It is free to download, with an optional one-time purchase (Vereda Full Access). It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your position. Vereda uses your location to show where you are and to guide you on a walk you started. It is processed on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • Your settings (including your hiking level), Wishlist, saved routes, saved hikes (their tracks, with times and positions) and imported files. They stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Whether you have Vereda Full Access. The app remembers it on your phone and checks it with Google Play.
  • If Google device backup is switched on, Android may include the app's settings, Wishlist, saved routes and saved hikes in your own backup. The offline map files are left out. That backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

Purchases

Vereda Full Access is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details, your name or your email. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Vereda Full Access, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new phone. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase.

What the app downloads

The app connects to these services to download public data. The requests carry no personal data, but like any internet request they show your device's IP address to the service:

  • GitHub (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com and GitHub's download servers): trail status, the offline map and app data. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
  • IPMA (api.ipma.pt): weather warnings.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org): the online map, used only until the offline map is downloaded.
  • Google Play: the price and your purchase of Vereda Full Access.

What you choose to share

  • Drive to start: the app opens Google Maps with the trail's start point. Google's privacy policy applies there.
  • SOS: when you call 112, or send or share your position by SMS or another app, it goes from your phone to the people you choose. We never receive it.
  • GPX files you export go wherever you send them.
  • Report a problem: opens your email app with the app version, your Android version and phone model, the offline map version, the age of the trail status and the last crash, if any. No position. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the email is only used to fix the problem.

Permissions

  • Location: only while the app is open, or while a walk you started is running (shown by a notification). The app never asks for background location.
  • Notifications: the walk notification and its alerts.
  • Internet and network state: to download the data above.
  • Vibration: walk alerts.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Vereda Full Access.

Children

Vereda is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the policy page, with a new date.
